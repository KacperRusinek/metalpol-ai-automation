# Business Understanding

## Company Context

Metalpol Sp. z o.o. is a manufacturing company producing metal components
for the automotive industry.

The complaint handling process has direct impact on:
- customer satisfaction,
- operational efficiency,
- production quality,
- quality assurance costs.

## Current Problems

### Operational Problems
- Manual complaint registration in Excel
- Delayed email processing
- High workload during seasonal peaks
- Lack of automation between systems

### Process Problems
- Inconsistent complaint categorization
- No standardized workflow
- SAP and JIRA disconnected

### Reporting Problems
- No KPI reporting
- No production line quality analytics
- Difficult root cause analysis

## Main Users

- Customer
- Service Specialist
- Quality Department
- Production Department
- CEO / Management

## Business Goals

- Reduce complaint response time
- Standardize categorization
- Automate repetitive tasks
- Improve reporting
- Reduce operational bottlenecks
